# Public-safe publication and evidence policy

- Entire organization, personas, transactions, data and sample numbers are fictional. No employer, client, proprietary brief, customer brand, Salesforce organization ID, ticket number or production metric is represented.
- Architectural patterns are generic and not purported to describe a deployment; no materials from a private project were copied.
- Distinguish **design recommendation** from **capability verified in official documentation** and from **validated local fixture**.
- Avoid screenshots of private systems; example IDs such as `ASG-1001` and domains such as `example.invalid` are intentionally non-operational.
- Verify licensing, supportability, API behavior, permissions, costs and deployment steps against current documentation and your own org before use.
- This playbook is educational, not legal, financial, compliance or security certification advice.
- Never put access tokens, secrets, credentials, data exports or private logs in issues, pull requests, examples, diagrams or screenshots.
- Do not represent hypothetical KPI improvements as real outcomes.
