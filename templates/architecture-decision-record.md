# ADR-NNN: [Decision title]

- **Status:** Proposed / Accepted / Replaced
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** Role, not individual contact details
- **Context:** Business goal, request volume, change frequency and sources
- **Requirements:** Data freshness, P95 target, budget, access, consent and RTO/RPO
- **Considered options:** A / B / C, with total cost, risk, effort, dependencies
- **Decision:** Choice and *why*, including when it no longer holds
- **Security:** Identity, entitlement, masking, audit and key management
- **Validation:** Evidence required, test cases and cutover gate
- **Risks and mitigations:** Failure modes and fallback
- **Operational owner:** Who monitors/reverts and how
- **Revisit trigger:** New capability, load, cost, latency or policy changes
