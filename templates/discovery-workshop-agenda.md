# Discovery workshop — 60 minutes (template)

- **0–10m:** sponsor business goal, baseline and non-goals.
- **10–20m:** support manager: top intents, exception rate, handoff behavior.
- **20–30m:** data owner: systems, IDs, update frequency, governance and SLAs.
- **30–40m:** security/privacy: authentication, delegated access, PII, audit, retention, approved knowledge.
- **40–50m:** technical team: existing APIs, incidents, integration constraints, channels.
- **50–60m:** agree pilot scope, measurable KPIs, owners, open questions and next decision gates.

**Outputs:** signed-off intent matrix; baseline measurement plan; red-line security constraints; source-of-truth inventory; ranked 2–3 MVP intents; unresolved risk/ADR list. A meeting is not a requirement sign-off until each decision has an accountable owner.
